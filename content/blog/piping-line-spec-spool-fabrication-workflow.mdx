Wrong **pipe schedule**, understated **wall thickness**, or a spool cut that ignores flange take-off and root gap shows up as rejected hydrotests, field rework, and delayed mechanical completion. This pillar walks the **piping spool fabrication workflow** from line class → B36 dimensions → B31.3 thickness → shop cut length → test pack—using the same numbers as FieldEngineersKit.

## Quick Summary (TL;DR)

- **Takeaway:** Lock NPS/OD from ASME B36.10M, size wall with ASME B31.3 $t_{\min}=t+c$ and $t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}=t_{\min}/0.875$, then cut spools with flange take-off and fit-up root gap before writing the hydrotest pack.
- **Governing relation:** $t = \dfrac{P D}{2(SE+PY)}$, $t_{\min}=t+c$, $t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}=t_{\min}/0.875$; catalog $ID = OD - 2t$.
- **Code / basis:** ASME B36.10M / B36.19M · ASME B31.3 Para. **304.1** · B31.3 Chapter VI test pressure · project Line Class Spec (LCS).

## Why a single piping line specification workflow matters

EPC piping packages still fail when **schedule tables**, **pressure design**, **spool cut sheets**, and **hydrotest** are treated as separate silos. A buyer who picks Sch 40 from habit, a stress engineer who forgets mill under-tolerance, and a shop that cuts to isometric face-to-face without weld gap will each “pass” their discipline and still produce a non-compliant spool.

Use the live [Pipe Schedule & Dimension](/calculator/pipe-schedule-dimension) calculator as the dimension source of truth for NPS → OD / $t$ / ID / plain-end weight, then pass the same OD into [ASME B31.3 Pipe Wall Thickness](/calculator/pipe-wall-thickness) for schedule acceptance.

## Core formulas & parameter definitions

### ASME B36.10M catalog geometry

$$
ID = OD - 2t,\qquad W_m = 0.0246615 \cdot t \cdot (OD - t)
$$

with $OD$, $t$ in **mm** and plain-end carbon-steel $W_m$ in **kg/m** at $\rho = 7{,}850\text{ kg/m}^3$ (FEK / B36 screening mass).

### ASME B31.3 pressure design thickness (Para. 304.1.2)

$$
t = \frac{P D}{2(SE + PY)}
$$

$$
t_{\min} = t + c,\qquad t_{\mathrm{nom,req}} = \frac{t_{\min}}{0.875}
$$

FEK symbols (do not mix with blind $t_m$): **$t$** = pressure design thickness; **$c$** = corrosion/mechanical allowance; **$t_{\min}=t+c$**; **$t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}=t_{\min}/0.875$** for 12.5% mill under-tolerance; **$t_{\mathrm{actual}}$** = selected schedule catalog wall.

### Parameter definitions

| Symbol | Parameter | Units | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **NPS / DN** | Nominal size | — | Tag only — not equal to OD for NPS ≤ 12 |
| **$OD$ / $D$** | Outside diameter | mm (in) | B36 catalog OD used in B31.3 $t$ |
| **$t$ (catalog)** | Nominal wall | mm (in) | Schedule wall from B36.10M / B36.19M |
| **$t$ (B31.3)** | Pressure design thickness | mm (in) | From $P D / 2(SE+PY)$ |
| **$t_{\min}$** | Minimum required with allowance | mm (in) | $t + c$ |
| **$t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}$** | Ordered thickness screen | mm (in) | $t_{\min}/0.875$ |
| **$P$** | Internal design pressure | MPa (psi) | Absolute design $P$ for thickness (FEK metric stores MPa) |
| **$S$** | Basic allowable stress | MPa (psi) | Table A-1 at design metal temperature |
| **$E$** | Quality factor | — | Seamless typically $1.00$; ERW often $0.85$ |
| **$Y$** | Coefficient | — | Ferritic steels through 482 °C: **0.4** in FEK |
| **$c$** | Corrosion allowance | mm (in) | From LCS (example below uses 1.6 mm) |

## Worked example — NPS 6 line class thickness and Sch 40 selection

### How do you run ASME B31.3 pipe wall thickness for an NPS 6 hydrocarbon line?

**Given (screening duty):**

| Input | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| NPS / material | NPS 6 · ASTM A106 Gr.B seamless ($E = 1.00$) |
| Line class cue | CS hydrocarbon (example LCS tag “A1A”-class duty) |
| Design pressure $P$ | **35 bar g = 3.50 MPa** |
| Design temperature | **120 °C** |
| Corrosion allowance $c$ | **1.6 mm** |
| $S$ (FEK A106-B preset) | **138 MPa** |
| $Y$ | **0.4** |
| OD (B36.10M / FEK) | **168.28 mm** |

Open the same duty in FEK: [Pipe Wall Thickness](/calculator/pipe-wall-thickness) with NPS 6, Sch 40, $P = 3.50\text{ MPa}$, $T = 120\text{ °C}$, $c = 1.6\text{ mm}$, material A106 Gr.B. Confirm OD on [Pipe Schedule & Dimension](/calculator/pipe-schedule-dimension/6inch-sch40) (or NPS 6 · Sch 40 in the tool).

#### Step 1 — Pressure design thickness $t$

$$
t = \frac{3.50 \times 168.28}{2\bigl(138\times 1 + 3.50\times 0.4\bigr)} = \frac{588.98}{2\times 139.4} = \mathbf{2.11\text{ mm}}
$$

#### Step 2 — Minimum required thickness $t_{\min}$

$$
t_{\min} = t + c = 2.11 + 1.6 = \mathbf{3.71\text{ mm}}
$$

#### Step 3 — Nominal ordered screen $t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}$ (12.5% mill)

$$
t_{\mathrm{nom,req}} = \frac{t_{\min}}{0.875} = \frac{3.71}{0.875} = \mathbf{4.24\text{ mm}}
$$

#### Step 4 — Select commercial schedule

| Schedule | Catalog $t_{\mathrm{actual}}$ | Mill min $0.875\,t_{\mathrm{actual}}$ | vs $t_{\min}$ / $t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}$ |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Sch 40 / STD | **7.11 mm** | **6.22 mm** | Pass ($6.22 > 3.71$ and $7.11 > 4.24$) |
| Sch 80 / XS | 10.97 mm | 9.60 mm | Pass (heavier — use only if LCS requires) |

**Selection:** **NPS 6 Schedule 40 (STD, 7.11 mm)** satisfies FEK B31.3 screening for this duty. Margin vs $t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}$: $(7.11/4.24 - 1)\times 100\% \approx +68\%$.

<Callout title="Why not “$t_{req} = 4.82$ mm”?" variant="tip">
Internet / LLM summaries sometimes quote a single “$t_{req}$” near **4.8 mm** for similar NPS 6 duties. In FEK symbols you must separate **$t$**, **$t_{\min}=t+c$**, and **$t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}=t_{\min}/0.875$**. For **$c = 1.6\text{ mm}$** at **3.50 MPa / 120 °C**, the engine asserts **$t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}\approx 4.24\text{ mm}$**, not 4.82 mm. Always recompute with the live calculator before freezing the LCS.
</Callout>

### Spool cut length & fit-up clearance (shop screening)

After schedule acceptance, shop cut length is **not** equal to isometric centerline length:

1. Read **flange face-to-face / take-out** from the isometric and [Flange Dimension / Weight](/calculator/flange-dimension-weight) (B16.5) or project MTO.
2. Confirm flange **class / facing** against [Flange Pressure-Temperature Rating](/calculator/flange-pressure-temperature-rating).
3. Add **fit-up root gap** per WPS (commonly on the order of **1.5–3 mm** per butt joint for CS open-root—**confirm WPS**; not a FEK engine output).
4. Subtract gasket / RTJ ring stack-up where the joint detail requires it.

$$
L_{\text{cut,screen}} \approx L_{\text{ISO,F-F}} - \sum L_{\text{flange take-off}} + \sum g_{\text{root}}
$$

Use this only as a **screening check** against the spool sheet. Final cut lists remain under the fabricator’s approved isometric BOM.

### Hydrotest before release

With design $P = 3.50\text{ MPa}$, draft the Chapter VI test pressure in [Hydro/Pneumatic Test Pressure](/calculator/hydro-test-pressure) using the line’s $S_t/S$ ratio—do not assume bare $1.5P$ when design temperature exceeds test temperature. Pair NDT (RT/UT/MT/PT) with the LCS examination table before packing the spool.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the ASME B31.3 pipe wall thickness calculation formula used in FEK?

FEK uses ASME B31.3 Para. 304.1.2:

$$
t = \frac{P D}{2(SE + PY)},\quad t_{\min}=t+c,\quad t_{\mathrm{nom,req}}=\frac{t_{\min}}{0.875}
$$

with ferritic $Y = 0.4$ through 482 °C in the screening preset. $D$ is **outside diameter** from B36, never NPS.

### How do I convert pipe schedule NPS to outer diameter and wall thickness?

Use the ASME B36.10M (CS/alloy) or B36.19M (S-schedules) catalog—or the [Pipe Schedule & Dimension](/calculator/pipe-schedule-dimension) calculator. NPS is a **label**; for NPS ≤ 12, OD is larger than the nominal inch (NPS 6 OD = **168.28 mm**, not 152.4 mm).

### What piping spool fit-up weld gap and fabrication tolerance should I use?

Root gap and high-low limits come from the **approved WPS/PQR** and project fabrication spec—not from B36 schedule tables. Common CS open-root gaps are often **~1.5–3 mm**, but treat that as shop folklore until the WPS confirms it. FEK does not compute weld gap; it supplies OD/wall and thickness acceptance so the cut sheet starts from the correct schedule.

### How does hydrostatic test pressure relate to ASME B31.3 allowable stress?

Minimum hydrotest pressure follows B31.3 Para. 345 with $P_t = 1.5\,P\,(S_t/S)$ (FEK screening), not bare $1.5P$, when design metal temperature exceeds test temperature. Compute $P_t$ in [Hydro/Pneumatic Test Pressure](/calculator/hydro-test-pressure) and see the dedicated [hydrotest guide](/docs/asme-b31-3-hydrostatic-pneumatic-test-pressure-guide).

### Why does NPS 10 Schedule 80 wall in FEK differ from some internet charts?

FEK’s B36.10M table lists NPS 10 Sch 80 as **15.09 mm (0.594 in)**. Charts that show **12.70 mm (0.500 in)** are mixing sizes/schedules (0.500 in is Sch 80 / XS on other NPS bands). Always trust the project B36 edition and the FEK loader over unmarked cheat-sheets.
