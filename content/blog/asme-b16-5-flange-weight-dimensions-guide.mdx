## 1. Core Formulas & Assembly Mass Calculation

$$
W_{\text{pair}} = 2 \cdot W_f + W_g + n \cdot \left( W_{\text{stud}} + 2 \cdot W_{\text{nut}} \right)
$$

$$
\text{PCD} = 2 \cdot R_{\text{bc}}
$$

### Parameter Definitions

| Parameter | Symbol | Engineering Unit | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Single Flange Mass** | $W_f$ | $\text{kg}$ ($\text{lb}$) | Bare catalog weight of forged flange (WN, SO, BL, SW). |
| **Gasket Mass** | $W_g$ | $\text{kg}$ ($\text{lb}$) | Metallic or spiral-wound gasket weight (B16.20-style screening). |
| **Fastener Set Mass** | $W_{\text{fasteners}}$ | $\text{kg}$ ($\text{lb}$) | Total weight of $n$ stud bolts and $2n$ heavy hex nuts. |
| **Pitch Circle Diameter** | $\text{PCD}$ | $\text{mm}$ ($\text{in}$) | Diameter of the bolt circle through hole centers. |
| **Bolt Hole Count / Size** | $n \times d_h$ | $\text{ea} \times \text{mm}$ | Hole count and diameter ($d_h = d_{\text{stud}} + 1/8$"). |

## 2. ASME B16.5 Dimensional Tolerances & Facing Specifications

### Raised Face (RF) Height Differences
* **Class 150 & 300**: Feature a $1.6\text{ mm}$ ($1/16"$) raised face, which is **included** inside the nominal flange thickness ($T_f$).
* **Class 600 through 2500**: Feature a $6.4\text{ mm}$ ($1/4"$) raised face, which is **added on top** of the minimum flange thickness ($T_f$).

### Key Standard Tolerances
* **Bolt Circle Diameter (PCD)**: Tolerance limit of $\pm 1.5\text{ mm}$ ($\pm 0.06"$).
* **Adjacent Bolt Hole Spacing**: Tolerance limit of $\pm 0.8\text{ mm}$ ($\pm 0.03"$).
* **Weld Neck Hub Bore**: $+0.8\text{ mm} / -0.0\text{ mm}$ for $\text{NPS} \le 10$ to limit internal step at the butt weld.

## 3. Step-by-Step Worked Example (NPS 6 Class 300 Joint)

### Field Problem Statement
Calculate envelope dimensions, stud bolting, wrench size, and total mated-pair assembly weight for an **NPS 6 (DN 150) Class 300 Weld Neck Raised Face (WN RF)** joint on Schedule 40 pipe.

Open the same duty in FEK: [Flange Dimension & Weight](/calculator/flange-dimension-weight) (NPS 6 · Class 300 · WN · RF).

### Step 1: Extract ASME B16.5 Dimensions
Per ASME B16.5 Table 11 for NPS 6 Class 300:
* **Flange Outside Diameter ($OD_f$)**: $317.5\text{ mm}$ ($12.50"$)
* **Flange Minimum Thickness ($T_f$)**: $36.5\text{ mm}$ ($1.44"$)
* **Hub Bore Diameter**: $154.06\text{ mm}$ (Matches NPS 6 Sch 40 ID)

### Step 2: Determine Bolting Layout & Tool Specs
Per ASME B16.5 Table 12:
* **Pitch Circle Diameter (PCD)**: $269.9\text{ mm}$ ($10.62"$)
* **Bolting Pattern**: $12 \times 3/4"$ studs ($L_{\text{stud}} = 120\text{ mm}$)
* **Bolt Hole Size ($d_h$)**: $22.2\text{ mm}$ ($7/8"$)
* **Wrench / Spanner Size**: $1-1/4"$ ($32\text{ mm}$) heavy hex nut socket

### Step 3: Calculate Bare Flange Weight (2 Flanges)
For a single NPS 6 Class 300 WN RF flange, $W_f \approx 26.50\text{ kg}$.

$$
W_{2f} = 2 \times 26.50\text{ kg} = 53.00\text{ kg}
$$

### Step 4: Calculate Fastener Mass (12 Studs + 24 Heavy Hex Nuts)
* Mass of one $3/4" \times 120\text{ mm}$ B7 stud $\approx 0.38\text{ kg}$.
* Mass of two $3/4"$ 2H heavy hex nuts $\approx 2 \times 0.15\text{ kg} = 0.30\text{ kg}$.
* Per stud assembly mass $= 0.68\text{ kg}$.

$$
W_{\text{fasteners}} = 12 \times 0.68\text{ kg} = 8.16\text{ kg}
$$

### Step 5: Total Mated-Pair Assembly Rigging Mass
Adding the spiral-wound gasket mass ($W_g \approx 0.35\text{ kg}$):

$$
W_{\text{pair}} = 53.00\text{ kg} + 0.35\text{ kg} + 8.16\text{ kg} = 61.51\text{ kg}
$$

**Conclusion**: Crane rigging and pipe rack load calculations must account for **$\approx 61.5\text{ kg}$** per joint for NPS 6 Class 300 WN RF.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does Class 150 mean the maximum pressure is 150 psi?
**No.** Pressure class is a dimensionless designator. Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP) depends on temperature and material group. Check the [Flange Pressure-Temperature Rating](/calculator/flange-pressure-temperature-rating) calculator for Group 1.1 / 2.2 screening.

### Q2. When must ASME B16.47 be used instead of ASME B16.5?
ASME B16.5 covers sizes up to **NPS 24**. For **NPS 26 to NPS 60**, use ASME B16.47. **Series A** and **Series B** have different bolt circles and are **not interchangeable**.

### Q3. Can I bolt a Flat Face (FF) flange directly to a Raised Face (RF) flange?
**Never mate carbon steel RF flanges to cast iron or bronze FF flanges** without removing the raised face or using a full-face gasket. The bending moment from the raised-face gap can crack brittle FF flanges.

### Q4. Why does the FEK pair weight differ from a single flange catalog sheet?
Catalog sheets often list **one** flange. Field lifts and rack loads need **two flanges + gasket + full stud/nut set** ($W_{\text{pair}}$). That is why internet “flange only” masses understate crane demand.
